Privacy Policy

Don't Pop It! - Balloon Game · Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NStudio Games ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Don't Pop It! - Balloon Game (the "Game"), available on Google Play under the package name com.nstudio.dontpopit.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create a separate account with us, and we do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Game settings and progress are stored locally on your device.

The Game uses two third-party services that process information about you: Google Play Games Services for leaderboards (see section 3) and Google AdMob for advertising (see section 2).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob and its partners may collect and use the following information to deliver, personalize, and measure ads, and to prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (ads viewed, clicked, and related diagnostics)

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)

The Game uses Google Play Games Services to provide a global leaderboard. When you sign in to Google Play Games and submit a score, Google processes the following information:

  • Your Play Games player ID, display name (gamer name), and profile image
  • Your score and the time it was submitted

Your display name, profile image, and score may be visible to other players on the leaderboard. We receive only the information Google Play Games Services provides to the Game, such as your player ID, display name, and score, and we use it to show the leaderboard and your rank. You can choose not to sign in to Google Play Games, in which case your scores will not be submitted to the leaderboard. You can manage your Play Games profile and its visibility in the Google Play Games app. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent: Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the Game's settings, where available.
  • Android settings: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. In-app purchases

If the Game offers in-app purchases (for example, gem packs and bundles of balloon skins and passengers), they are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or other payment details. We may receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so that we can unlock the purchased item.

6. How we use information

We use locally stored data to run the Game and save your progress, and we use leaderboard data to display rankings. We do not sell your personal information. Information collected by Google through advertising and Play Games Services is used by Google as described in sections 2 and 3.

7. Sharing of information

We do not share personal information with anyone except the services described above (Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services, and Google Play), which process data under their own policies, or where required by law. Your leaderboard name, profile image, and score are visible to other players as described in section 3.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We do not keep our own server-side profile about you. Leaderboard data is stored by Google Play Games Services. You can manage or delete your Play Games data and profile through the Google Play Games app and your Google Account's privacy controls. If you would like help with removing your score from the leaderboard, contact us at the email address below.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the limited data the Game handles. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page with a new effective date at the top. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact NStudio Games at nstudiogames.dev@gmail.com.
