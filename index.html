Privacy Policy

Don't Pop It! - Balloon Game · Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NStudio Games ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Don't Pop It! - Balloon Game (the "Game"), available on Google Play under the package name com.nstudio.dontpopit.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Game progress such as scores and settings is stored locally on your device.

The Game shows advertising provided by a third party (see section 2). That service may automatically collect certain information from your device, as described below.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob and its partners may collect and use the following information to deliver, personalize, and measure ads, and to prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (ads viewed, clicked, and related diagnostics)

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices about ads

  • Consent: Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the Game's settings, where available.
  • Android settings: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. In-app purchases

If the Game offers in-app purchases (for example, to remove ads), they are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or other payment details. We may receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so that we can unlock the purchased item.

5. How we use information

We use locally stored data only to run the Game and save your progress. We do not sell your personal information. Information collected by advertising partners is used by them as described in section 2.

6. Sharing of information

We do not share personal information with anyone except the advertising and payment services described above, which process data as independent controllers under their own policies, or where required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We do not keep a server-side profile about you. For data held by Google, please see Google's privacy policy and your Google Account's privacy controls.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the limited data the Game handles. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page with a new effective date at the top. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
